Neun neue Blockwelt-Szenen, erstellt mit der integrierten Bildgenerierung.

Stilbrief: moderne räumliche Voxelwelt, detailreiche quadratische Materialien, natürliches Licht, Schatten und atmosphärische Tiefe; freundlicher Held mit braunem Haar, türkisfarbenem Oberteil und situationsabhängiger Ausrüstung.

## start
The young blocky adventurer with short brown cuboid hair, teal tunic and navy trousers arrives on an empty grassy meadow, carrying a small backpack, looking across pristine land, sunrise, no building yet.

## home
Same brown-haired blocky adventurer in teal tunic actively building a cozy timber cabin, crouching to place a wooden block beside the front door, scaffolding and stacked wood, warm afternoon light.

## food
Same brown-haired blocky adventurer in teal tunic seated on a log beside a campfire eating a loaf of bread, steaming pot, cabin in background, intimate cozy dusk scene.

## leather
Same brown-haired blocky adventurer putting on warm brown leather armor inside a rustic cabin beside a workbench and mirror, dynamic three-quarter view, lantern lighting.

## wood
Same brown-haired blocky adventurer wearing leather armor crafting a wooden sword at an outdoor workbench, wood shavings, cabin behind, bright natural light.

## firstbattle
Same brown-haired blocky adventurer wearing reflective silver iron armor and holding wooden sword in dynamic guarded stance battling a friendly fantasy moss-covered stone golem in forest clearing, no gore, shafts of sunlight.

## iron
Same brown-haired blocky adventurer wearing silver iron armor forging a shining iron sword at a glowing forge, hammer striking anvil, sparks, dramatic orange and blue lighting.

## crystal
Same brown-haired blocky adventurer wearing luminous turquoise crystal armor and iron sword exploring a beautiful cavern of crystalline blocks, admiring giant glowing crystals, reflections and volumetric rays.

## champion
Same brown-haired blocky adventurer wearing turquoise crystal armor lifting matching crystal sword victoriously on a hill above the protected block village, banners and warm sunrise, cinematic heroic wide shot.
